FAQ

Before you book.

If your question is not covered here, message us with your location and service type.

What should I send when asking for a quote?

Your locality, property or room type, preferred day, areas that need cleaning, and a few current photos when possible.

Do you bring cleaning products and equipment?

Product and equipment requirements are confirmed when the booking is discussed. Tell us what is already available at the property so there is no misunderstanding.

Can I request recurring cleaning?

Recurring arrangements may be available for suitable locations and schedules. Ask about weekly or periodic availability when you message.

Are the website prices fixed?

No. They are starting guides. Your confirmed price depends on the actual scope, condition, size, access and location.

Do you cover all of Malta?

The main focus is central Malta and nearby localities. Other locations can be considered depending on schedule and travel time.
