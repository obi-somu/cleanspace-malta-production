Privacy

Privacy information

CleanSpace Malta uses the information you voluntarily provide when you contact the service to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, arrange cleaning appointments and communicate about an agreed service.

Information you may provide

This can include your name, phone number, locality or property location, preferred appointment time, service details and photos you choose to send for quoting purposes.

How information is used

Information is used for service communication, quoting, scheduling and carrying out the requested cleaning service. This website does not operate an online customer account system.

Third-party communication

If you contact CleanSpace Malta through WhatsApp, phone or another third-party platform, that platform's own privacy terms also apply.

Website data

This website does not currently use a booking form, advertising cookies or third-party analytics. If those features are introduced later, this notice should be updated accordingly.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions related to an enquiry or booking, contact CleanSpace Malta through the same business contact channel used for the service.
