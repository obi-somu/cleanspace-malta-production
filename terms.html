Service terms

CleanSpace Malta provides cleaning by appointment. The service scope, price, location and timing should be agreed before the appointment.

The customer is responsible for providing safe, reasonable access to the property at the agreed time.

Personal belongings

Customers should secure valuables, sensitive documents and fragile items before the cleaning appointment.

Please give as much notice as possible if you need to change or cancel an appointment.
