Service terms

Basic booking and service terms

These terms explain the practical basis on which CleanSpace Malta accepts cleaning enquiries and appointments. Specific details agreed in writing for an individual booking take priority where appropriate.

Quotes and pricing

Website prices are starting guides only. A final quote may depend on location, property size, current condition, access, requested tasks and estimated time. The agreed price and scope should be confirmed before the appointment.

Access and preparation

The customer is responsible for ensuring safe and reasonable access to the property and identifying fragile, sensitive or restricted items or areas before cleaning starts.

Service scope

Only tasks included in the agreed scope are assumed to be part of the booking. Additional work may require extra time, a revised price or a separate appointment.

Products and equipment

Cleaning product and equipment arrangements should be confirmed during booking. Known sensitivities, material restrictions or product preferences should be disclosed in advance.

Changes and cancellation

If you need to change or cancel an appointment, provide as much notice as reasonably possible. CleanSpace Malta may also need to rearrange an appointment if illness, transport, safety or another significant issue prevents the service from being carried out.

Availability

All services are subject to confirmed availability and service area. Sending an enquiry does not by itself create a confirmed booking.

Last updated: August 2026. These practical terms should be reviewed as the service grows and formal business requirements are finalised.
